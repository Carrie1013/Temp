01 / About

A working
conference
for advisors.

Two days built around one thesis: markets are uncertain, and portfolios must be designed to deliver real outcomes across that uncertainty. Day one examines how portfolios should be built. Day two translates that into practice — how advisors actually deliver across households, tax conditions, and evolving markets.

01

Quantitative Rigor

Michaud Optimization, statistical portfolio construction, and the methodology behind decisions that stand up over time.

02

Advisor Reality

Household-level decision-making. How real advisors coordinate accounts, taxes, and evolving market conditions.

03

Technology in Practice

FrontierOne, AI in wealth management, tokenization, and the infrastructure reshaping portfolio delivery.

04

Signal Over Noise

Rapid-fire crossfire on what actually matters — stripping away the industry noise and tying ideas back to outcomes.

Day One · May 11

The Investment Case

Optimization and rebalancing methodology. The ETF ecosystem. Tax-aware construction. Global market perspective. The emerging infrastructure of tokenization. The rigorous case for how decisions should be made — then dinner on the harbor.

Day Two · May 12

Delivering in Practice

FrontierOne through a real client's lens. Outcome-based advice at scale. Global models. The evolving advisor role. Private assets. AI in wealth management. Market Crossfire to close — what actually matters for long-term outcomes.
